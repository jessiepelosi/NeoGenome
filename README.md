# <i>Neomusotima</i> Genome
Assembly, annotation, and analysis of the <i>Neomusotima conspurcatalis</i> genome. 

In preparation for study of the <i>Neomusotima conspurcatalis</i> genome, a biocontrol agent of the invasive fern <i>Lygodium microphyllum</i>. This work was funded by the United States Department of Agriculture NIFA Postdoctoral Fellowship Award #2024-67012-43394. 

<b>Authors:</b> Jessie A. Pelosi, Taylor R. Curry, Abby R. Pearse, Melissa C. Smith, and Katrina M. Dlugosch
